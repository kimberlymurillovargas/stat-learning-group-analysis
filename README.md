# stat-learning-group-analysis

Overall, the analysis shows that professional sanctions are related to the legal 
field of the case. In particular, criminal and immigration cases showed higher 
odds of a reported professional sanction compared with contract cases. However, 
this does not mean that the legal field itself causes a professional sanction. 
It only shows an association within the cases included in this dataset.

The results also show the importance of being careful when working with this 
data. Some legal fields had very few cases, so we combined the smaller categories
to make the regression more reliable. Since this dataset is based on reported 
AI hallucination cases, the results also cannot be used to represent all AI 
hallucinations or legal cases more generally.

Overall, this analysis suggests that the context in which an AI hallucination 
occurs may matter when looking at professional sanctions, but there are still 
limitations to what we can conclude from this dataset.This further shows that 
many lawyers have been caught submitting court filing hallucinated by AI and to
not generalize to a larger population.
